Click-to-Queue for YouTube

One mindless, simple click.

Those lazy days, or busy nights, you just want a playlist to run in the background- except, instead of zoning out or zoning in, you've found yourself painfully queueing up videos; load the feed, click the video's 'more options', add to queue- if you misclick, you are taken to another page, and now you've lost your video. A painful process- one which should really be a feature or a simple 1 click... one mindless simple click- so that you can carry on with yourself...

    Why a folder and not one click? Chrome only does one-click installs from the Chrome Web Store. Until it's listed there, Chrome loads it from a folder on your computer. The code is short and readable, and the only permission it asks for is storage.

    What it does

    Everything happens in your browser. No account, no API key, no tracking.

    One click to queue

    With Queue Mode on, a click on any thumbnail or title queues it. Click again to take it out. Nothing navigates away.

    A playlist for every day

    Each pick is filed under today's date. Replay or copy any day later, even after you clear the queue.

    Play all

    Plays the whole queue as one YouTube playlist, up to YouTube's limit of 50 videos.

    Sees YouTube's own queue

    Videos you add with YouTube's "Add to queue" get the same red frame, so you can see what's lined up.

    Keyboard first

    Alt+Q toggles Queue Mode, Esc closes the panel, and Ctrl or Cmd-click still opens a new tab.

    Private by design

    One permission: storage. Your queue never leaves your browser.
